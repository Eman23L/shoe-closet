How listing works

From your cupboard to the shop

  1. Fill in the form. Name, sizes, condition and a fair price.
  2. Add a photo. Put the photo in the images folder and type its file name.
  3. Copy the entry. Paste it into shoes.js and save.
  4. Refresh the site. Your pair appears in the shop straight away.
