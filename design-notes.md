# Design notes (patterns copied)

Note: web search returned only third-party scraper pages, not layout docs, so these notes
come from common patterns on StockX, Vinted, GOAT and similar resale sites.

- Header: sticky top bar with logo left, nav (Home, Shop, Sell, About) and basket link right.
- Home: big hero banner with headline and one "Shop now" button.
- Home: category tiles (Trainers, Boots, Heels, Sandals, Kids) linking to the shop pre-filtered.
- Home: "Featured shoes" row of cards; "how it works" / trust strip (checked, secure, easy returns).
- Shop: filter sidebar on the left (brand, size, max price, condition), stacks above grid on phones.
- Shop: result count plus sort dropdown (price low-high, high-low, newest) above the grid.
- Cards: square image, price prominent, brand, name, size and condition underneath.
- Product page: large image left; right column has brand, name, price, condition badge.
- Product page: size buttons (one selected), colour, "Add to basket" button, description.
- Product page: trust points under the button, and a "You may also like" row of 4 shoes.
- Footer: short about text, links to every page, contact placeholder, copyright.
- Look: keep the playful rainbow header strip, coloured card edges, green price pills.
